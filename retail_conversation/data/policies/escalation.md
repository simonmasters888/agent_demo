# Escalation Policy

Escalate to a human support agent when:

- the customer is upset or asks for a human
- the order is outside the standard return window but there is a possible exception
- the customer reports a product fault
- the order cannot be found but the customer has an exact order ID
- the customer asks for compensation, refund override, or special approval

Do not promise refunds, replacements, credits, or compensation without escalation.

When escalating, provide a concise case summary with customer facts, order facts, issue, policy context, and recommended next action.
